## The Code
The Blinky If Project required me to turn lights on and off in a specific sequence. First, they blinked in order: yellow, red, green and blue. The blue light lasts 10 seconds, then yellow turns on. After that, they would turn on every specific multiple of seconds (yellow every 5, red every 6, green ever 7, and blue every 8. And they would stay on until another light turned on. To differentiate the moment before blue's 10 second streak and after, I used a true/false statement.

Sometimes, in the second phase, the count would have multiple factors, for example, 24. This would turn on red (pin 6) and blue (pin 8) at the same time, and they would both turn off at 25 for yellow (pin 5). Below is a picture of the red and blue lights on at the same time.
