Welcome to a special mini post from your favorite evil snake! Today's project had us using buttons to turn on a random light, which would stay on until the button is deactivated. Since my button is currently imbedded into my plushy, I had to use alligator clips, which is unfortunate because they don't connect very well. The LilyPad board detects every minor movement and usually activates the lights multiple times during the process of touching the metal clips on and off the board. So is the light sequence truly without consecutive repeats? I hope so. "___"
