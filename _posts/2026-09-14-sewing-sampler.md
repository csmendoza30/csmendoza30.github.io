This project included the whip stitch, running stitch ( + running stitch with small spaces), back stitch, cross stitch and blanket stitch. The two stitches I decided to add are the chain stitch (making up the purple heart) and the fly stitch (making up the turquoise feather). I am especially proud of the feather because I had a lot of fun changing the stitch sizes to create the shape and including spaces within the feather barbs to make it look more natural.

I have two pieces of advice for future me. For one, it's good to keep the last few stitches a little slack before starting to secure the thread because that will make it easier. This is especially useful for the satin stitch and running stitch with small spaces. It's hard to pass the needle through a previous stitch when they're all extremely short on the wrong side. When they were too taut, I often ended up stabbing my needle straight through the fabric. My second piece of advice is to give myself more time. I always underestimate how long an art project can take. Starting sooner, or at least spreading out the work, will make the process more manageable and more enjoyable as a whole.
