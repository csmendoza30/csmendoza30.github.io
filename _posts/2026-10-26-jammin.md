Why must the snake charmer be depicted as a human playing music to a snake? Snakes can be charming too! Why, I'll prove it. Behold, my tunes!

In this project, we took a song and played it through the buzzer on LilyPad boards. When a button (A9 pin) is pressed, the song plays and stops when the button is released. To code the notes, I assigned each note the right frequency (which I found [here](https://electricalelibrary.com/en/2018/08/26/arduino-tutorial-part-9-music-and-keypad/) on Electricale Library). The song I chose was "Creator" by Lena Raine, one of my favorite Minecraft OSTs (original soundtracks). The song is long and complicated so I only managed to code the first minute of it. I worked through this assignment by listening to "Creator" on Spotify while using a virtual piano in another tab to help me isolate the notes. 

One of the first things I tried to do was play multiple notes by switching between them quickly (like how we turned the LEDs to different brightnesses) but the buzzer couldn't keep up with that. So instead I played around with the tempo to give the illusion that multiple instruments were playing at the same time. I split the song into ten chunks, each with an if statement checking to see if the button was pressed before continuing the song. I also made a new variable "section" to help keep track of which chunk was supposed to play. This help me test chunks individually, and it insured that when the button was pressed, the song would always start at the beginning.
